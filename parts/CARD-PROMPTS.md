# Card portrait prompts

One portrait per supporting player, shown on his card after a big play.
Keep the full-size originals in `parts/_cards_full/` (not published). The game
loads card-size JPEGs from `parts/cards/<name>.jpg` (760 x 456, quality 88,
about 40 KB each instead of 500); a PNG dropped straight into `parts/cards/`
also works. Until a file exists the card shows the drawn player instead.

## Rules for every portrait

- **Size and shape:** landscape, 5 by 3 (for example 1140 x 684). The card
  crops to that shape, so keep the head and shoulders centred.
- **Style:** flat vector cartoon, hard edges, bold shapes, no gradients, no
  soft shading, no text, no logos, no watermark. Same look as the players.
- **Background:** one solid colour filling the whole image (given per
  character). It is not keyed out, so do not use magenta here.
- **Kit:** navy `#1f2d54` jersey and shoulder pads with gold `#c8a63c` trim.
  No real team logos.
- **Public domain only:** draw each character from the original myth or book.
  No film or cartoon designs: no Universal-style monster (flat-topped head,
  neck bolts), no film-style caped count, no Disney-style Pan (green tunic
  with red feather), no Marvel or DC look.

# Action scene prompts (the new cards)

These replace the portraits. Each one shows the character pulling off his
signature move and taking over the picture, with ordinary players around him.
Save each one under the same file name as its portrait (for example
`king-arthur.png`) in `parts/_cards_full/`. The card-size JPEG goes in
`parts/cards/` at 800 x 600.

## Rules for every action scene

- **Size and shape:** 4 by 3 landscape (for example 1200 x 900). Keep
  anything important out of the outer 5%, because the card frame covers it.
- **Style:** bold cartoon, like a mobile football card game. Thick black
  outlines, bright saturated colours, simple cel shading, exaggerated
  proportions and poses, lots of motion lines and energy effects. No text,
  no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
- **Who is in it:** the character is big and in front, in the middle of his
  move, and fills about half the picture. Two to four ordinary players in plain
  red jerseys and red helmets (no numbers, not based on any real person) are
  around him, knocked back, reaching, falling or chasing. They are smaller and
  sit behind him or at the edges.
- **No uniform on him:** he looks like himself from the story (fur, armour,
  robes, bandages), not a football player. The only football thing on him is
  the ball. The red-jersey players are the only ones in football gear.
- **Background:** a packed stadium at night under bright lights, blurred and
  simple so he stands out.
- **Public domain only:** the same rules as the portraits. No film or
  cartoon designs.
